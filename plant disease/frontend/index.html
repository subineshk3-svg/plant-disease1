Photography Guidelines

How to achieve 95%+ diagnostic accuracy

  • Focus on Symptoms: Capture visible lesions, spots, yellowing rings, or powdery patches directly.
  • Natural Indirect Light: Avoid severe shadow casting or extreme flashlight glare wash-out.
  • Steady Focus: Ensure sharp margins. AgriScan AI automatically rejects excessively blurry images.
  • Isolate the Plant: Keep non-plant background objects (fingers, tools, pavement) minimized.

Diagnostic Integrity Guarantee

AgriScan AI is engineered with strict safeguards: if an uploaded image lacks sufficient foliar evidence or presents inconclusive signals, the system outputs an Unknown / Uncertain category rather than fabricating a disease prediction.
